# EU AI Act timeline

Status as of 11 November 2026. Generated from `data/milestones.json`; do not edit by hand.

## 1 August 2024: The AI Act enters into force

Status: in force.

Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 enters into force. Nothing is yet enforceable; the dates below start the clock on each group of rules.

Applies to: Provider of an AI system, Deployer of an AI system, Provider of a general-purpose AI model.

Articles: 113.

Sources: [Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (the AI Act), EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj)

## 2 February 2025: Prohibited practices and AI literacy apply

Status: in force.

Chapters I and II apply: the Article 5 prohibitions and the Article 4 AI literacy duty. Article 4 has since been amended; the consolidated text frames the provider and deployer duty as taking measures that support AI literacy, with no required level for any individual.

Applies to: Provider of an AI system, Deployer of an AI system.

Articles: 4, 5.

Digital Omnibus: amended by the Omnibus.

Sources: [Article 113, entry into force and application, consolidated text in the AI Act Explorer](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/113/); [Digital Omnibus amendments in the AI Act Explorer](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/ai-act-explorer/digital-omnibus/)

## 2 August 2025: General-purpose AI obligations, governance and penalties apply

Status: in force.

Chapter V (general-purpose AI models), Chapter VII (governance), Chapter XII (penalties), notified body rules in Chapter III Section 4 and Article 78 apply, except Article 101.

Applies to: Provider of a general-purpose AI model, Member State authority.

Articles: 51, 52, 53, 54, 55, 56, 78, 99.

Sources: [Article 113, entry into force and application, consolidated text in the AI Act Explorer](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/113/)

## 27 July 2026: The Digital Omnibus on AI enters into force

Status: in force.

Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 amends the AI Act, signed on 8 July 2026. It moves the high-risk dates and adds the new Article 5 prohibitions.

Applies to: Provider of an AI system, Deployer of an AI system, Provider of a general-purpose AI model.

Articles: 113.

Digital Omnibus: added by the Omnibus.

Sources: [Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (Digital Omnibus on AI), EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2026/1744/oj)

## 2 August 2026: General application date: transparency duties and Commission enforcement over general-purpose AI

Status: in force.

The general application date in Article 113 arrives. Article 50 transparency duties apply, subject to the Article 50(2) transition below. Article 101, the fines for providers of general-purpose AI models, applies from this date because it was excluded from 2 August 2025.

Applies to: Provider of an AI system, Deployer of an AI system, Provider of a general-purpose AI model.

Articles: 50, 101.

Sources: [Article 113, entry into force and application, consolidated text in the AI Act Explorer](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/113/); [Article 111, AI systems and models already placed on the market, consolidated text in the AI Act Explorer](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/111/)

## 2 December 2026: Deadline for Article 50(2) marking on systems already on the market

Status: in 21 days.

Providers of generative systems placed on the market before 2 August 2026 must comply with the Article 50(2) duty to mark synthetic output by this date. Systems placed on the market on or after 2 August 2026 get no grace period.

Applies to: Provider of an AI system.

Articles: 50, 111.

Digital Omnibus: added by the Omnibus.

Sources: [Article 111, AI systems and models already placed on the market, consolidated text in the AI Act Explorer](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/111/); [Digital Omnibus amendments in the AI Act Explorer](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/ai-act-explorer/digital-omnibus/)

## 2 December 2026: New Article 5 prohibitions apply

Status: in 21 days.

The new prohibitions added by the Omnibus, covering AI systems that generate non-consensual intimate imagery and child sexual abuse material, apply from this date.

Applies to: Provider of an AI system, Deployer of an AI system.

Articles: 5.

Digital Omnibus: added by the Omnibus.

Sources: [Article 113, entry into force and application, consolidated text in the AI Act Explorer](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/113/); [Digital Omnibus amendments in the AI Act Explorer](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/ai-act-explorer/digital-omnibus/)

## 2 August 2027: General-purpose AI models placed on the market before 2 August 2025 must comply

Status: in 264 days.

Providers of general-purpose AI models that were on the market before 2 August 2025 must take the necessary steps to comply by this date.

Applies to: Provider of a general-purpose AI model.

Articles: 53, 55, 111.

Sources: [Article 111, AI systems and models already placed on the market, consolidated text in the AI Act Explorer](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/111/)

## 2 December 2027: High-risk obligations apply to Annex III use cases

Status: in 386 days.

Chapter III Sections 1 to 3 apply to AI systems classified as high-risk under Article 6(2) and Annex III, such as employment, education, credit scoring and essential services. Moved by the Omnibus from 2 August 2026.

Applies to: Provider of an AI system, Deployer of an AI system.

Articles: 6, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 26, 27.

Digital Omnibus: moved by the Omnibus.

Sources: [Article 113, entry into force and application, consolidated text in the AI Act Explorer](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/113/); [Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (Digital Omnibus on AI), EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2026/1744/oj)

## 2 August 2028: High-risk obligations apply to AI in Annex I regulated products

Status: in 630 days.

Chapter III Sections 1 to 3 apply to AI systems classified as high-risk under Article 6(1) and Annex I, such as safety components of regulated products. Moved by the Omnibus from 2 August 2027.

Applies to: Provider of an AI system, Deployer of an AI system.

Articles: 6, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17.

Digital Omnibus: moved by the Omnibus.

Sources: [Article 113, entry into force and application, consolidated text in the AI Act Explorer](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/113/); [Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 (Digital Omnibus on AI), EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2026/1744/oj)

## 2 August 2030: High-risk systems for public authorities already on the market must comply

Status: in 1360 days.

High-risk AI systems intended to be used by public authorities and placed on the market before the high-risk rules apply must be brought into compliance by this date.

Applies to: Provider of an AI system, Deployer of an AI system.

Articles: 111.

Sources: [Article 111, AI systems and models already placed on the market, consolidated text in the AI Act Explorer](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/111/)

## 31 December 2030: AI components of large-scale IT systems must comply

Status: in 1511 days.

AI systems that are components of the large-scale IT systems listed in Annex X, placed on the market or put into service before 2 August 2027, must be brought into compliance by this date.

Applies to: Provider of an AI system, Deployer of an AI system.

Articles: 111.

Sources: [Article 111, AI systems and models already placed on the market, consolidated text in the AI Act Explorer](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/111/)
